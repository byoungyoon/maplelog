# Visual Assets

## Background

- Active files: `public/backgrounds/meadow-dusk.mp4` and `meadow-dusk-poster.jpg`.
- Source: [Maciej Cisowski — Clouds Moving over Meadow / Pexels](https://www.pexels.com/video/clouds-moving-over-meadow-13732087/).
- License: [Pexels License](https://www.pexels.com/license/).
- The recorded landscape shows a distant forest, meadow, and sunset sky. The web asset is silent 1080p video; the poster is extracted from the video, not an application screenshot.
- The video asset was slowed to 0.3x and converted to 30 fps. The browser plays that asset at normal speed.
- Background motion can be paused and also stops for reduced-motion preferences and inactive tabs. A skippable 2.6-second entrance runs on initial ledger entry.

## Logo

- User-provided original: `public/brand/logo-original.png`.
- Active sage glass variant: `public/brand/icon-sage.png` and `src/app/icon.png`.
- Created with ImageGen in image-editing mode. The design brief retained the original maple coin and ledger motif, using sage green, soft gold, and transparency. The site icon excludes the wordmark.

## Bosses and Rewards

`public/catalog` contains 124 images from MapleScouter's public boss and reward data. Source URLs and verification dates are in `src/data/scouter-catalog.json` and the [API contract](api-contract.md). Game imagery remains the property of its respective rights holders.
